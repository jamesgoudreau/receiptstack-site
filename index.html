Receipt Stack

Snap a receipt. It's read, categorized, and filed in your cloud.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

📸 Scan

The built-in document scanner finds the edges, straightens the page, and snaps automatically.

🔎 Read

On-device text recognition pre-fills vendor, date, tax, and total — and suggests a category. You confirm.

☁️ File

The receipt lands in your own Google Drive or iCloud, organized by year and month, with a yearly spreadsheet index.

Capture from anywhere

Share sheet

Send PDFs or photos straight into Receipt Stack from Mail, Files, or Photos.

One press away

Long-press the icon, assign the Action Button, or ask Siri to scan a receipt.

Duplicate warning

Scanned the same receipt twice? The app notices and tells you before saving.

Your data stays yours

No account to create. No tracking. No servers of ours in the middle. Text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone, and files are saved only to the cloud storage you choose — folders you can open and browse yourself. The app never deletes or overwrites a saved receipt.

Built for anyone who has ever lost a receipt they needed at tax time, to return a product, or for warranty service.
