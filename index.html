What we build

Receipt Stack

An iPhone app that reads a receipt on the device, then files the image and a yearly spreadsheet index in your own Google Drive or iCloud. We never host the files.

Receipt Stack product page

How we work

On the device first

Text recognition for receipts runs on your phone. We do not send images to a Maritime Digital server to be read.

Your storage, not ours

Saves go to the Google Drive or iCloud Drive account you connect. We cannot open that archive from our side.

No product accounts

Receipt Stack has no Maritime Digital login, no analytics product, and no advertising SDK.

Built in PEI

The company is based in Prince Edward Island. Support is by email at the domain above.
