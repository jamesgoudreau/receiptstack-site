About Maritime Digital Solutions

Maritime Digital Solutions is a Canadian software company based in Prince Edward Island. The public site and products use the shorter name Maritime Digital. Both refer to the same organization.

We make tools for people who already have a place to keep files. The first product is Receipt Stack, a receipt scanner that files into the user's own Google Drive or iCloud Drive.

What the company does

We design, build, and support consumer software. We do not run a Receipt Stack backend. Receipt images and the yearly index never pass through our servers, and we cannot open them.

The work is done in Prince Edward Island. Customers reach us at info@maritimedigital.ca, an address on this domain.

Product

Receipt Stack reads vendor, date, amounts, and tax on the iPhone, then saves the image and a spreadsheet or CSV index into cloud storage the user controls. There is no Maritime Digital account to create.
