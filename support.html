Support

Receipt Stack saves scanned receipts to your own Google Drive or iCloud Drive, with a yearly spreadsheet index.

Where are my receipts?

In the cloud storage you connected: Google Drive → Receipts folder (with a Google Sheets index per year), or iCloud Drive → Receipt Stack → Receipts (with a CSV index). The buttons at the bottom of the app's home screen take you straight there.

A save said the index will "sync in the background". Is my receipt safe?

Yes. The file uploaded successfully; only the spreadsheet row is queued and will be added automatically next time the app opens with a connection.

How do I switch between Google Drive and iCloud?

Tap the gear icon on the home screen.

How do I scan faster?

Long-press the app icon → Scan receipt, or assign Scan Receipt to your Action Button (Settings → Action Button → Shortcut).
